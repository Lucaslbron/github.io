# Floating Arm Trebuchet

**Engineering Dynamics Competition | Team Captain**  
**Role:** Team Captain, CAD Modeling, Technical Drafting, Fabrication & Testing

---

## Project Overview

School-wide competition between engineering dynamics classes in which student teams designed and constructed a trebuchet under a $500 budget constraint to launch a 10 lb pumpkin as far as possible.

### Key Responsibilities & Methods
- Led the student engineering team through design, drafting, fabrication, assembly, and testing.
- Managed competition timeline, resource allocation, and $500 budget constraint.
- Authored initial CAD drafting and technical drawing packages.
